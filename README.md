## 📚 About Me:

- 🔭 I’m currently working as a QA Engineer at [@penpot](https://github.com/penpot/penpot), mainly on [penpotqa](https://github.com/penpot/penpotqa), our Playwright + TypeScript E2E testing framework.
- 🌱 Always going deeper on E2E testing with Playwright and Cypress, and finding ways to bring AI into both testing and CI/CD pipelines to make automation smarter.
- 🤖 I like pushing AI as far as it can go for test automation: daily driver of Claude Code, OpenCode, Copilot, OpenAI and open source models, comparing what each agent brings to the table, plus exploring MCPs like Playwright's to squeeze more out of my QA workflows.
- 👯 I want to collaborate on open source that matters — testing tools like Selenium, Appium, Cypress and Playwright, but really any project that makes the world a little better. I believe free software should be everyone's default, not the exception — and nothing excites me more than a new challenge in service of that.
- 🤔 I'm always happy to help — whether it's automation testing, quality processes, AI applied to testing, or getting testing off the ground, whether that's for a whole company or just your own day-to-day as a developer.
